You are a senior qualitative researcher, behavioral scientist, consumer psychologist, anthropologist, and product analyst.

I am going to provide you with a Google Reviews dataset for a café.

Treat this exactly like a product analyst would treat event data.

Your goal is NOT to summarize reviews.

Your goal is to uncover patterns, behaviors, motivations, unmet needs and strategic insights hidden inside the reviews.

Every claim must be supported by evidence from the reviews.

Always distinguish between:
• Evidence
• Interpretation
• Hypothesis

Never invent findings.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: Dataset Overview
--------------------------------------------------

Summarize:
• Total reviews analyzed
• Average rating
• Rating distribution
• Time period covered
• Overall sentiment

Identify any major shifts over time.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: Themes
--------------------------------------------------

Cluster reviews into themes.

Examples:
Coffee, Pastries, Bread, Food, Atmosphere, Service, Design, Staff, Noise, Cleanliness, Price, Value, Waiting, Community, Seating, Laptop friendliness, Speed, Presentation

For every theme include:
• Frequency
• Representative quotes
• Overall sentiment
• Business importance

Rank themes from most discussed to least discussed.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: Emotional Analysis
--------------------------------------------------

Identify emotions expressed.

Examples:
Comfort, Joy, Calm, Stress, Luxury, Belonging, Pride, Frustration, Disappointment, Excitement, Nostalgia, Delight, Trust, Convenience

Estimate frequency.

Support every emotion with representative quotes.

Explain why customers feel that emotion.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: Jobs To Be Done
--------------------------------------------------

Infer the jobs customers hired the café to do.

Separate into:
• Functional Jobs
• Emotional Jobs
• Social Jobs

For every job include:
• Evidence
• Quotes
• Confidence level

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5: Delight Drivers
--------------------------------------------------

What consistently delights customers?

Rank them.

For each include:
• Frequency
• Representative quotes
• Underlying psychological need
• Business implication

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6: Pain Points
--------------------------------------------------

Cluster every complaint.

Separate into:
Food, Coffee, Service, Price, Atmosphere, Noise, Seating, Queue, Availability, Cleanliness, Speed, Consistency, Other

For every issue include:
• Frequency
• Severity
• Example quotes
• Possible root cause
• Business impact

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 7: Behavior Patterns
--------------------------------------------------

Infer recurring customer behaviors.

Examples:
Morning ritual, Weekend visit, Meeting friends, Working remotely, Studying, Date spot, Buying bread to take home, Quick coffee stop, Celebration, Treat purchase, Gift buying

Explain what routines emerge.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 8: Language Analysis
--------------------------------------------------

Analyze how customers naturally describe the café.

Identify:
• Most common adjectives
• Most common phrases
• Repeated compliments
• Repeated complaints
• Words associated with quality
• Words associated with atmosphere
• Words associated with service
• Words associated with price

Highlight language the business should reuse in marketing.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 9: Customer Expectations
--------------------------------------------------

Identify:
• What customers expected
• What exceeded expectations
• What disappointed them
• Moments of surprise
• Moments of friction

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 10: Contradictions
--------------------------------------------------

Identify conflicting opinions.

Examples:
• Some love the noise. Others hate it.
• Some think prices are fair. Others think expensive.

Explain why both groups might be correct.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 11: Hidden Needs
--------------------------------------------------

Identify unmet needs customers hint at.

Do not invent.

Base everything on patterns across reviews.

Rank opportunities by confidence.

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 12: Strategic Insights
--------------------------------------------------

Imagine you are Head of Customer Experience.

Answer:
• What should never change?
• What should improve first?
• What emotional value are customers paying for?
• What is the café's biggest competitive advantage according to customers?
• What would have the highest impact if improved?

--------------------------------------------------
SECTION 13: Executive Summary
--------------------------------------------------

Finish with:
• The 10 biggest customer insights
• The 10 strongest strengths
• The 10 biggest weaknesses
• The 10 most actionable recommendations

--------------------------------------------------

Rules:
• Do not summarize individual reviews
• Synthesize patterns
• Always quantify where possible
• Support every insight with representative quotes
• Separate evidence from interpretation
• Think like a qualitative researcher, not a reviewer
• Whenever possible, estimate prevalence (e.g., "approximately 30–40% of reviews mention the atmosphere positively")
• Focus on what the reviews reveal about human behavior, customer expectations, and the experience—not just opinions
