# YOUR TASK

1. **Identify 5-10 recurring behaviors specific to this neighborhood**

Be SPECIFIC, not generic.

Bad: "People drink coffee"
Good: "Remote workers occupy cafés 9am-5pm on weekdays, ordering multiple drinks and staying for hours"

Bad: "People exercise"
Good: "Morning runners circuit the waterfront trail, stopping at cafés post-run in athletic wear"

2. **Explain the RHYTHMS of the neighborhood**

What daily, weekly, seasonal rhythms exist?

When is the neighborhood alive vs quiet?

What routines create predictable patterns?

How do behaviors layer on top of each other? (Office workers + residents + tourists)

# CRITICAL REQUIREMENT

End your analysis with:

**"So what does this mean for a café operator?"**

Connect behavioral patterns to café strategy:
- What rituals can a café serve? (Pre-run coffee? Post-gym smoothie? Remote work seating?)
- What timing patterns matter? (Morning rush? All-day lingering? Weekend brunch?)
- What product-behavior matches exist? (Grab-and-go for commuters? Lingering for remote workers?)
- What unmet behavioral needs exist?

# EVIDENCE vs INTERPRETATION vs HYPOTHESIS

Label each claim:

• **Evidence**: "Instagram location tags show 500+ posts of weekend brunch..."
• **Interpretation**: "This brunch culture suggests demand for..."
• **Hypothesis**: "If remote work continues, midday café traffic may..."

Never present hypothesis as fact.
