# EVIDENCE vs INTERPRETATION vs HYPOTHESIS

Label each claim:

• **Evidence**: "Approved development at X address will add 500 units by 2028..."
• **Interpretation**: "This population growth likely means..."
• **Hypothesis**: "If current trends continue, the neighborhood may..."

Distinguish between:
- **Certain**: Approved, funded, under construction
- **Likely**: Proposed, historical pattern suggests
- **Possible**: Plausible but uncertain
