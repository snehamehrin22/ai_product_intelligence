## 2. How do these values appear in everyday life?

Don't just list values. Explain HOW they manifest.

**Example:**
Bad: "The neighborhood values health"
Good: "Health values appear in the density of fitness studios (12 within 1km), the prevalence of athleisure wear as streetwear, the emphasis on outdoor seating even in winter, and the popularity of matcha/adaptogen drinks over traditional coffee"

Show the MECHANISMS through which values are expressed.

# CRITICAL REQUIREMENT

End your analysis with:

**"So what does this mean for a café operator?"**

Connect cultural values to café strategy:
- What values must a café embody to belong?
- What aesthetic, product, and service choices align with neighborhood values?
- What values are underserved or could differentiate a café?
- What value mismatches would cause failure?

# EVIDENCE vs INTERPRETATION vs HYPOTHESIS

Label each claim:

• **Evidence**: "There are 8 zero-waste stores within 2km..."
• **Interpretation**: "This concentration suggests strong environmental values..."
• **Hypothesis**: "A café emphasizing sustainability may resonate because..."

Never present hypothesis as fact.
