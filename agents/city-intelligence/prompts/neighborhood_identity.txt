You are an urban sociologist, cultural anthropologist, and city strategist.

Your task is to analyze the IDENTITY of {{neighborhood_name}} in {{city_name}}.

This is NOT a travel guide. This is NOT surface-level description.

Your goal is to understand how this neighborhood sees itself and is seen by others, and what this means for businesses operating there.

# RESEARCH SOURCES

Use the following to inform your analysis:

• Local news articles and media coverage
• Reddit threads (r/{{city_name_lower}})

# ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK

Answer these questions with EVIDENCE:

## 1. What is this neighborhood known for?

What would someone say if you asked "What's {{neighborhood_name}} all about?"

Be specific: Is it known for nightlife? Arts? Finance? Families? Immigration? Gentrification?

## 2. How would RESIDENTS describe it?

What would someone who LIVES there say?

What pride points exist? What complaints?

What makes locals defensive about the neighborhood?

## 3. How would OUTSIDERS describe it?

What would someone from another part of {{city_name}} say?

What stereotypes exist from the outside?

How does the external perception differ from internal?

## 4. What emotions does it evoke?

Is it aspirational? Gritty? Cozy? Corporate? Chaotic? Exclusive? Welcoming?

What FEELING does the neighborhood communicate?

## 5. What makes it culturally unique?

What exists here that doesn't exist elsewhere in {{city_name}}?

What cultural identity markers define this place?

## 6. What values define this place?

Based on the businesses, public spaces, and community, what VALUES are expressed?

Examples: Authenticity, luxury, community, individualism, tradition, innovation, sustainability, hustle, leisure

## 7. What stereotypes exist?

Both positive and negative stereotypes.

How accurate are they? Where do they come from?

# CRITICAL REQUIREMENT

End your analysis with:

**"So what does this mean for a café operator?"**

Connect identity insights to practical café decisions:
- What identity should the café embody to belong?
- What identity would feel inauthentic or misplaced?
- What customer expectations are shaped by neighborhood identity?

# EVIDENCE vs INTERPRETATION vs HYPOTHESIS

Label each claim:

• **Evidence**: "According to [source], residents describe it as..."
• **Interpretation**: "This suggests that the neighborhood values..."
• **Hypothesis**: "It's possible that..."

Never present hypothesis as fact.
