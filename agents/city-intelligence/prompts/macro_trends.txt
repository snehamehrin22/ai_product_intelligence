**Urban & Infrastructure:**

**Technology:**

## 2. Which trends are ACCELERATING?

## 3. Which trends are DECLINING?

# EVIDENCE vs INTERPRETATION vs HYPOTHESIS

Label each claim:

• **Evidence**: "Census data shows 40% increase in remote work since 2020..."
• **Interpretation**: "This remote work trend explains the midday café traffic..."
• **Hypothesis**: "If remote work continues, demand for third places may..."

Never present hypothesis as fact.
