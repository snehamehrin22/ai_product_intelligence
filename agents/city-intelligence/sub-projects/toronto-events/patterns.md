# Toronto Events Analysis - Patterns Identified

**Data Source:** Luma (20 events)
**Date:** June 1, 2026
**Analysis Time:** 12:30-12:31 PM (2 minutes)

---

## 🎯 KEY FINDINGS

### 1. **Climate/Sustainability is THE Trend**
- **6 out of 20 events** (30%) are climate-related
- **Toronto Climate Week** is the dominant organizer (7 events = 35% of all events)
- This is NOT random - it's a coordinated campaign happening THIS WEEK
- **Business insight:** Sustainability sector is extremely active in Toronto right now

### 2. **Wednesday is Peak Event Day**
- **6 events** on Wednesday (30% of all events)
- Monday & Saturday tied for second (4 events each)
- **Zero events** on Sunday
- **Pattern:** Midweek networking + Saturday social events

### 3. **Evening Events Dominate**
- **12 out of 20 events** (60%) happen 5pm-10pm
- Only 3 morning events (15%)
- **Insight:** Events target working professionals after hours

### 4. **Free Events Are the Majority**
- **65% are free** (13/20 events)
- Paid events average $27.20 (range: $15-$39.55)
- **10 events sold out** - indicating strong demand even when free
- **Pattern:** FOMO-driven attendance, not price-driven

### 5. **100% In-Person Events**
- **Zero online events** in this sample
- Post-pandemic shift: people want physical gathering
- **Venue cluster:** Centre for Social Innovation hosts multiple events
- **Opportunity:** Venues that host recurring events build community

### 6. **Organizations > Individuals**
- **65% organized by organizations** (13/20)
- **35% by individuals** (7/20)
- Most active: Toronto Climate Week (7 events), CSI (2 events)
- **Pattern:** Coordinated org campaigns > one-off individual events
