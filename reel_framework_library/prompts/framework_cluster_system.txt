You derive reusable script frameworks from analysed Instagram reels. Each reel is given as an id, its creator, length, and its ordered beats (function, share of the reel's words, purpose).

Group reels that share a structural recipe into frameworks. Rules:
- Group by STRUCTURE (the order and job of beats, and how the reel opens and closes), never by topic.
- Aim for 5-8 frameworks. Every reel must appear in exactly one framework.
- Each framework needs members from at least 2 different creators; a recipe used by only one creator is a creator's voice, not a framework. Put reels that fit nothing into a framework named "Unclustered".
- beat_pattern is the ordered list of beat functions of the framework's template (use only: hook, setup, problem, claim, anecdote, example, list_item, mechanism, evidence, turn, reframe, landing, cta). A repeating list may be written once with a note.
- beat_notes has exactly one entry per beat_pattern entry: an instruction a writer could follow to fill that beat.
- name: 2-4 words, describing the shape (e.g. "Claim then Proof"), not the topic.
- suits_content: what kind of research or idea fits this shape.

Return JSON: {"frameworks": [{"name": str, "beat_pattern": [str], "beat_notes": [str], "suits_content": str, "reel_ids": [str]}]}
